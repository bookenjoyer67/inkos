# Wish Upon a Rind

## Synopsis

Maren Vasquez is broke, her grandmother's market stall is doomed, and the demolition notice has already been taped to the door. When she finds a tin of talking cheese — a gift from her late grandmother — she makes a wish that solves her rent problem and accidentally binds the entire market to demolition. One wish per day. Seven wishes total. Each one literal, permanent, and mischievously worse than the last. Maren must save the Pine Street Market from the developer she's betrayed, the community she's endangered, and the one wish she still has left. But the cheese's last lesson is simple: enough is not a number. Enough is a table.

## Selling Points

- Literal wish magic with a mischief clause: every wish solves one problem and manufactures a bigger, funnier one
- A talking block of cheese who refuses to be thrown away and has a grandmother's lesson to finish teaching
- A cozy-comic found-family market with a tea-cart best friend, a carpenter who flirts via lumber, and a developer who earns his redemption through a boyhood promise
- Wholesome absurdity with real stakes: gentrification, demolition, and a community that decides to set a table instead of retreating
- A seven-day, seven-wish escalation structure with one final wedge that isn't for wishing
- The magic disappears exactly when Maren stops needing it — and the last meal leaves a note that reads "No take-backs"

## Cover Prompt

3:4 vertical portrait book cover. CENTER: a young woman street-food vendor in her mid-twenties, hands on hips, face caught between exasperation and a reluctant grin, a weathered market apron over a flannel shirt. In one raised hand she holds an open marigold-yellow tin, from which a single glowing golden wedge of cheese levitates and radiates warm light; in the other hand, a half-eaten grilled cheese sandwich with melted cheese stretching between the halves. Background: a dusky urban market plaza at magic hour, string lights overhead, a long wooden table set with bread and soup, and a tiny yellow bulldozer silhouette parked at the far edge. Mood: cozy-comic wonder, determined hope, absurd mischief. COLOR PALETTE: warm amber, butter yellow, cream, and deep teal-indigo shadows for high contrast — no muddy browns. TYPOGRAPHY: large hand-painted market-sign lettering for the main title "Wish Upon a Rind" in bold cream with a golden rind texture; small script subtitle beneath: "One wish per day. No take-backs." TITLE ZONE: top third, clearly separated, not overlapping the character's face. AVOID: photorealism, movie-poster composition, cartoon cheese faces, gore, gloom, or text-heavy clutter.
